Week5 reflection

A reflection on our research process and the challenges of working with data from multiple reports.

The reflection

Process & challenges

We examined how British university students use AI for their assignments, categorising the findings into several themes, including AI tools, patterns of use, benefits and risks, and emotional impacts.

One of the most challenging parts was translating the data into a shared framework. Each report used different definitions and variable names, so we had to manually reclassify and summarise the findings, which was time-consuming. For data that were difficult to scrape, we adapted our approach and provided a more general summary.
